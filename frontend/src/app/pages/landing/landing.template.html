What we believe

Five principles.
No exceptions.

These aren't aspirations. They're architecture. Every feature, every parameter, every rule flows from one of these five commitments.

01

Real value creation

Every dollar of profit comes from actual buy/sell transactions. The platform has no obligation to pay returns it hasn't earned.

02

Meritocratic reputation

Signal quality matters. Contributors who find profitable opportunities earn more and gain privileges. Bad signals lose reputation and get throttled.

03

Radical transparency

Every opportunity is visible with full financial breakdown. Every action is recorded as an event. No black-box accounting, ever.

04

No recruitment dependency

Growth comes from finding better opportunities, not recruiting more members. A smaller group with better signals outperforms a larger group with mediocre ones.

05

AI assists, humans decide

Automated checks support vetting. The community votes. Money and reputation are never handed to an algorithm — that's a hard architectural line.
