How changes work

  1. Edit a parameter and click Propose change.
  2. A Governance Vote opens — VETTER+ members cast reputation-weighted votes.
  3. On pass, the change is recorded with provenance and applies to future executions only.
  4. Integrity safety rails (reconciliation, no-ponzi, human control, KYC) cannot be voted.
